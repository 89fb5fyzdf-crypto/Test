How stories are ranked

Four times a day, articles from the last 36 hours are collected. Articles about the same story are grouped, then ranked by topic (funding round, acquisition, unicorn…), outlet reliability, how many outlets cover it and how recent it is. European Tech excludes France and puts the largest rounds first. French Tech stories stay in French; European Tech stories are in English.
